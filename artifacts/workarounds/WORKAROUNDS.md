# Step 5 — Workaround Mining

Workarounds are read here as *evidence of friction*, not as automatic proof of opportunity. For
each, the final column states what the workaround reveals that a naive solution would miss.

---

## PS-015 / PS-004 — Procurement

| Workaround | Who uses it | Compensates for | Cost | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sleeping at the mandi for 2–3 days beside the load** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE) | farmers without own transport | no credible arrival time; no guarantee the load is safe if left | transport + labour + food, sometimes exceeding the MSP premium | The farmer is paying to *hold a position in a queue*. The queue position, not the information, is the scarce good. |
| **Unloading paddy onto public roads** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE) | farmers at congested centres | exhausted mandi space | spoilage and weather risk | Physical capacity, not scheduling, is the binding constraint. |
| **Selling to the private trader at the gate instead** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, Nashik) | farmers facing rejection risk | slow grading + rejection after transport | accepts a lower price for immediate certainty | **The competing product is not a better queue — it is certainty.** Farmers already trade money for certainty. This prices the opportunity. |
| **Routing everything through the arhtiya** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE) | most farmers | credit gap between seasons, plus mandi access and sequencing | 15–24% interest vs 8–10% institutional | The intermediary bundles credit + access. Unbundling only the access half leaves the farmer still captured by the credit half. |
| **Arhtiyas hoarding / trading e-tokens** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE: uneven allocation, tokens for mandis where the agent does not operate) | commission agents | centralised allocation blind to local reality | farmer's access mediated | A digital token, once scarce, becomes an asset the intermediary captures. **Any new scheduling token will be captured the same way unless allocation is bound to the physical load.** |
| **State broadcasting "do not bring high-moisture paddy"** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE) | state agencies | inability to observe grain condition before arrival | pushes measurement burden onto a party with no instrument | An instruction that cannot be complied with. The clearest unmet need found. |
| **Sending a sample ahead with a relative/agent before moving the truck** | INFERENCE — plausible and consistent with the arhtiya request to test at mandi entry; **not directly evidenced** | rejection risk | a trip | Flagged as UNKNOWN. Worth one phone call to confirm; would strongly support the pre-commitment framing. |

## PS-032 — Informal recycling

| Workaround | Who uses it | Compensates for | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cash, memory and paper slips for every transaction** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE: informality is the norm; ~70% of kabadiwalas have no smartphone) | collectors and aggregators | no ledger, no bank rails, low literacy assumptions | Any design requiring the collector to operate an app fails for the majority of the population it claims to serve. |
| **Self-declared documentation for EPR certificates** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, CPCB) | registered recyclers | absence of independent verification of collection | The workaround *is* the fraud vector. 600,000 fake certificates were produced through it. |
| **Buying credits rather than collecting** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE) | brands / PIBOs | inability to run their own collection | Brands are already paying cash for *evidence*. A market for proof exists and is transacting. It is currently buying bad proof. |
| **NGO-mediated collector registration** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, Mongabay) | NGOs and municipalities | collector mistrust of the state | Trust is brokered by an intermediary the collector already knows. A system with no trusted broker will not get enrolment. |

## PS-023 — Transit safety

| Workaround | Who uses it | Compensates for | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Not reporting at all (~93% of incidents)** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, n=57 victims, 4 complaints) | most victims | distrust of authorities, stigma, futility | The dominant "workaround" is silence. Any funnel starting at *victim reports* starts at ~7%. |
| **Travelling only in the ladies' compartment / at particular hours / with company** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, high prevalence literature) | women commuters | absence of reliable in-transit response | Women already run a private routing and scheduling algorithm. Its inputs are experiential, not published — the richest untapped signal in this problem. |
| **Sharing live location with family** | INFERENCE — widespread, not specifically evidenced here | absence of institutional response | Trust is placed in personal networks, never in the operator. |

## PS-028 — Windshield

| Workaround | Who uses it | Compensates for | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper checklist / supervisor sign-off** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE: standard poka-yoke context) | line supervisors | no automatic record that all points were pressed | Confirms the demand for *completion evidence*, which is the easy half. |
| **Downstream water-leak test to catch bad bonds** (INFERENCE from process description) | QA | inability to verify bond quality at the station | The defect is found late and expensively. Value is in moving detection to the station — which is exactly what the statement asks. |
| **Torque/force tools with counters on adjacent stations** (SECONDARY_EVIDENCE, poka-yoke practice) | assembly lines generally | operator force variation | **The industry's existing answer to "was force adequate" is to instrument the tool, not to watch the hand.** Vision-only force verification is a departure from established practice and needs justification, not assumption. |
