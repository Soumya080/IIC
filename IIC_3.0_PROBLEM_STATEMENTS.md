### PS-028 — Build an AI powered windshield installation guidance system 

**Category:** Transportation & Mobility

**Problem Statement:**
Using cameras and computer vision algorithms, the system will identify the windshield area and display predefined pressure application points on the windshield or on the monitor or augmented display. Operators will be guided to sequentially apply pressure (with adequate pressure) at each designated location. The system will track completion of all required points and provide immediate confirmation once the standard process has been followed correctly
